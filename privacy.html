Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

Nestless is a personal-use home-automation project maintained by boland3333-ux. This policy describes how the app handles information.

What we collect

Nothing. Nestless does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the developer or any third party.

Your data stays with you

Any data the app touches — including camera images, account information, and any content accessed through your Google account — remains on your own devices and within your own Google account. It is not sent to the developer and is not backed up to any server we operate.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, the app uses those credentials only on your device to access your own resources. Google user data is not stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere by us, and is not used for advertising, analytics, or resale.

Children

Nestless is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
